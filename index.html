A private household organizer

Coordinate everyday life with control over your information.

AdministrateMe helps invited household members organize schedules, tasks and requests with a Chief of Staff assistant, called CoS. Each member uses their own private Console and decides which connected services the application may read or write.

Connect the Google services you choose

In the member Console, open Settings → Connections → Add Google account. Review the account and select Read and Write access for each supported service before signing in with Google.

  • Gmail: read new mail; optionally enable supported sending and mail-management actions.
  • Google Calendar: read calendars and events; optionally enable event creation and changes.
  • Google Tasks: read lists and tasks; optionally enable task and list changes.

Write access requires Read access. Connection status and permission controls remain available in Settings. Broader permissions require a fresh review with Google.

Decide what CoS can use and share

A Google connection does not automatically give CoS access to a member’s private information or share that information with another household member. CoS access depends on the member’s separate delegation and source permissions. Coordination and sharing follow the household’s configured permissions.

Enabling Write does not approve a particular message or external action. Sending still requires the application’s action approval, separate OpenClaw approval, and applicable recipient and privacy checks.

Obligation intake uses requests and material members send or forward to CoS for review. This Google connection flow keeps automatic obligation extraction from personal inboxes off.

Understand how information is handled

AdministrateMe stores synchronized records in local encrypted history. Authorized assistant work may send permitted context to the AI services configured through OpenClaw. Turning a service’s Read permission off stops further acquisition and CoS source access but preserves existing history.

Read the privacy notice for details about Google data, assistant processing, retention and revoking access.
